While you wait

What to do right now.

These steps limit the damage before we arrive.

  1. 01

    Turn off the water

    Close the stopcock. It is usually under the kitchen sink or where the water pipe enters the house.

  2. 02

    Make electrics safe

    If water is near sockets, lights or the consumer unit, switch off the electricity at the consumer unit if it is safe to reach.

  3. 03

    Drain and contain

    Open the cold taps to drain the pipes, catch drips in buckets and move valuables away from the water.

  4. 04

    Call the mobile

    Ring 07872 584 324, day or night, and tell us what you can see.

What should I do while I wait for an emergency plumber?

Turn off the water at your stopcock, which is usually under the kitchen sink or where the water pipe enters the house. If water is near electrics, switch off the electricity at the consumer unit if it is safe to do so. Open the cold taps to drain the pipes, and catch drips in buckets.

Do you take on small plumbing jobs?

Yes. Replacement taps, leaking pipes, toilets, showers and radiators are all welcome. No job is too small.
